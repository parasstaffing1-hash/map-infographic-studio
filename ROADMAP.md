# Roadmap

1. Foundation: FastAPI service, PostGIS migration runner, entity graph APIs, and ingestion quality reports.
2. Uttarakhand political data: verified current constituency versions, elections, candidates, results, filters, and historical comparison.
3. Editor: persistent map projects, annotations, label placement, data binding, and revision history.
4. Scale: vector tiles/PMTiles, national administrative coverage, lazy layer loading, uploads, choropleths, and deck.gl overlays.
5. Sharing/export: read-only share viewer, embeds, PNG/SVG/PDF, attribution pipeline, and infographic templates.
6. Collaboration: workspaces, permissions, classifications, and audit trail.
